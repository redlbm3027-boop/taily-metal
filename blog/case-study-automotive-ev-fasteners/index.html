Case Study: Custom Cold Headed Fasteners for EV Battery Pack Assembly

Aug 12, 2026 • Case Study

The Challenge

A leading Tier 1 automotive supplier approached Taily Metal with a critical requirement for a new electric vehicle (EV) battery pack assembly. The project demanded a custom-designed, non-standard bolt that could provide both structural integrity (Grade 10.9) and high corrosion resistance for a service life exceeding 15 years.

Key specifications included:

  • Material: SCM435 Alloy Steel
  • Strength Class: 10.9 per ISO 898-1
  • Plating: Zinc-Nickel (Zn-Ni) for 1000+ hours salt spray resistance
  • Tolerance: Critical head dimensions within ±0.05mm

The Taily Metal Solution

Initially, the client considered CNC machining due to the complex head geometry. However, our engineering team proposed a **multi-station cold heading process**. By optimizing the die design, we were able to form the complex head shape in a single operation.

Process Advantages:

  • Cost Reduction: 35% lower unit cost compared to CNC machining.
  • Strength: Cold heading maintains the grain flow of the metal, resulting in superior fatigue resistance compared to cut threads.
  • Waste Reduction: Material waste was reduced from 40% (CNC) to less than 2% (Cold Heading).

Result & Impact

Within 10 days, we delivered functional samples that passed all mechanical and chemical tests. The final production run of 500,000 units was delivered ahead of schedule, enabling the client to meet their SOP (Start of Production) milestone.

"Taily Metal's ability to convert our CNC design into a high-efficiency cold heading process saved us significant time and budget. Their quality reporting is top-tier." — Procurement Manager, EV Systems
