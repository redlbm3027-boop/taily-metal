Case Study: Custom Precision Stainless Steel Fasteners for Medical Diagnostic Imaging Equipment

Aug 12, 2026 • Case Study

The Challenge

A global medical device manufacturer required a custom shoulder bolt for a high-precision MRI scanner mechanism. The part required 316L stainless steel for non-magnetic properties and biocompatibility, along with extremely tight tolerances on the shoulder diameter.

Technical Requirements:

  • Material: Stainless Steel 316L
  • Tolerance: Shoulder diameter ±0.015mm
  • Surface Finish: Ra 0.4 (Mirror finish)
  • Quantity: 50,000 pieces per annum

The Taily Metal Solution

Due to the material toughness and tolerance requirements, many suppliers quoted full CNC machining, which was prohibitively expensive. Taily Metal implemented a **hybrid manufacturing approach**.

Hybrid Strategy:

  • Step 1 (Cold Heading): Form the primary blank and head shape using multi-station cold heading. This achieved 90% of the geometry at high speed.
  • Step 2 (Precision Grinding/CNC): Secondary precision grinding was used to achieve the ±0.015mm shoulder tolerance and Ra 0.4 finish.
  • Step 3 (Quality Control): 100% automated optical inspection (AOI) for critical dimensions.

Result & Impact

The hybrid approach delivered a **45% cost saving** compared to the client's previous full-CNC supplier. The reduction in cycle time also allowed us to maintain a stable JIT (Just-In-Time) inventory for the client, reducing their supply chain risk.

"Taily Metal's engineering team really understood our technical constraints. The combination of cold heading and secondary finishing was the perfect balance of precision and economy." — Head of Engineering, Medical Systems
