Case Study: Enhancing Durability for High-End Ergonomic Office Furniture

How Taily Metal delivered custom high-precision brass fasteners for a leading European standing desk brand, reducing assembly line failures by 22%.

2026-08-24 7 min read
Home/Blog/Article

The Challenge: Aesthetic Meets Strength

A premier European ergonomic furniture manufacturer was facing a dual challenge: their standing desk assemblies required screws that could withstand repetitive motor-driven vibrations while maintaining a flawless, premium aesthetic for the luxury market.

The Taily Metal Diagnosis

Our engineering team identified that standard cold-headed screws had minor concentricity issues, leading to "driver cam-out" (slipping) during high-torque automated assembly, which scratched the expensive wood surfaces.

The Solution: Precision Brass Engineering

We developed a custom manufacturing route using tungsten carbide dies to ensure a tolerance of ±0.02mm on the hex socket depth. Additionally, we applied a specialized PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) brass finish that passed 96-hour salt spray tests without losing its metallic luster.

The Result: Zero-Defect Assembly

The switch to Taily Metal fasteners resulted in a 22% increase in assembly line speed and a 100% reduction in surface damage claims. The client now uses our precision hardware for their entire global product line.
