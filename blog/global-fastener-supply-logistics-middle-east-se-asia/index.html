The Pivot to Emerging Markets

The global manufacturing landscape is shifting. At Taily Metal, we have optimized our production and logistics to serve high-growth regions including the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Central Asia, Russia, Mexico, and Australia. These markets demand more than just "low cost"—they require specific standard compliance and robust logistics.

Meeting Regional Standards: Beyond ISO 9001

While ISO 9001:2015 is our foundation, we specialize in manufacturing to regional standards critical for success in these areas: DIN (Germany/Russia/Europe), JIS (Japan/Southeast Asia), and AS/NZS (Australia/New Zealand). This ensures that every bolt we ship is "drop-in ready" for local engineering specifications.

Logistics Optimization: FOB, CIF, and DDP

Exporting to Africa or the Middle East requires deep knowledge of customs and maritime routes. Leveraging our proximity to Dongguan and Shenzhen ports, we provide flexible shipping terms. For clients in Mexico and Central Asia, we handle complex multi-modal transport to ensure on-time delivery despite geographical challenges.

Strategic Advantage: Precision Cold Heading at Scale

With an annual capacity of 500M+ pieces and a 15,000+ sqm facility, we are equipped to support the massive infrastructure and industrial growth in regions like Saudi Arabia (Vision 2030) and the booming electronics sector in Vietnam and Thailand.
