Expert Material Insights

Carbon Steel

The workhorse. Always pair with zinc, galvanizing or Dacromet finish.

Stainless Steel 304

Most common stainless. Resists rust without plating.

Stainless Steel 316

Adds molybdenum for maximum corrosion resistance.

Engineering Decision Tip

"Most fastener failures aren't due to poor material, but poor pairing of material + finish for the target environment."
