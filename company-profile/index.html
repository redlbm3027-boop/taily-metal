Who We Are

20+ Years of
Fastener Excellence

Started from a workshop in 2001, the 1st factory founded in 2013, the 2nd factory founded in 2018. Today we are a globally recognized supplier serving automotive, aerospace, construction, and industrial clients across more than 50 countries.

Our 15000+ sqm facility is built around high-speed cold heading production, supported by CNC machining and stamping capabilities, with a team of 100+ skilled professionals delivering over 500 million precision parts annually.

What We Do

Core Manufacturing

Cold Heading

Our core process. Multi-station cold heading machines produce precision bolts, screws, and custom fasteners at high volume.

CNC Machining

Complementary CNC turning and milling for complex geometries, special shapes, and secondary operations.

Stamping

Stamping capabilities for custom parts, brackets, and sheet metal components with tight tolerances.
