Overview

Many OEMs spend thousands of hours sorting, kitting, and packing fasteners. Our assembly lines turn that into a managed service: we kit, bag, label, and barcode fastener sets to your BOM, so parts arrive on your line ready to install.

From simple poly-bagged kits to complex sub-assemblies with multiple components, Taily Metal delivers build-to-print solutions with JIT scheduling and global logistics (FOB, CIF, DDP).
