Fastener & Product FAQs

Quick answers on standards, samples, tolerances, and custom quoting.

Which fastener standards do you produce to?+

We produce to DIN, ISO, ANSI/ASME, GB, and JIS standards, and we build to custom drawings (build-to-print) for non-standard parts.

Can you match a sample part I send?+

Yes. Send us the physical sample or photos with measurements, and we will reverse-engineer the part, recommend material and finish, and produce matching pieces.

Do you offer heat treatment and surface finishing?+

Yes. We provide heat treatment for strength grades up to 12.9, plus zinc plating, hot-dip galvanizing, Dacromet, black oxide, nickel plating, and passivation — all with our in-house or certified partner processes.

What tolerances can you hold?+

CNC-machined features are held to +/- 0.01 mm; stamped and cold-headed features typically +/- 0.05 mm. Tighter tolerances are possible for critical dimensions — confirm on your drawing.

How do I get a quote for custom fasteners?+

Send your drawing (PDF, DXF, or 3D) and target quantity to cherry@tl-screw.com or use the contact form. We reply within 24 hours with pricing, tooling cost, sample lead time, and production schedule.

Do you provide inspection reports with shipments?+

Yes. Every shipment can include material certificates and inspection reports covering dimensions, hardness, tensile strength, and salt-spray results, per your quality agreement.
