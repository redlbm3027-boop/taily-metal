Overview

Cold heading (cold forming) is the backbone of our production line. Wire rod is fed into multi-station heading machines and formed into fasteners at high speed, without heat é¥?which preserves material strength, delivers excellent surface finish, and keeps unit costs low for high-volume runs.

With 20+ years of experience and over 80 production machines, Taily Metal produces hex bolts, carriage bolts, flange bolts, machine screws, self-tapping screws, studs, and custom cold-headed parts from M3 to M64, in grades from 4.8 to 12.9.
