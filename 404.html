Frequently Asked Questions

Everything buyers usually ask before placing a custom fastener order.

What materials do you work with?+

We manufacture fasteners and precision parts in carbon steel, stainless steel (304/316), alloy steel, brass, aluminum, and titanium — with plating options including zinc, hot-dip galvanizing, Dacromet, black oxide, and nickel.

What size range do you produce?+

Our cold heading and CNC lines cover M3 to M64 in metric sizes (approximately #2 to 2-1/2" imperial), in strength grades from 4.8 to 12.9, per DIN, ISO, ANSI, GB, and JIS standards or custom drawings.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?+

MOQ depends on the part and process. We accommodate trial orders and low-volume runs to help you validate before scaling — send your drawing and target quantity for a specific answer.

How fast are samples and production lead times?+

Custom samples are typically delivered within 7-10 days. Production lead time depends on quantity and process, typically 15-30 days after sample approval. We provide a firm schedule with every quotation.

Are you ISO certified?+

Yes. Taily Metal is ISO 9001:2015 certified, with dimensional, hardness, tensile, and salt-spray testing across our quality control process. Every shipment can include inspection reports.

Can you manufacture from my drawing?+

Absolutely. We are an OEM/ODM manufacturer with 20+ years of custom experience. Send a PDF, DXF, or 3D file and our engineers will review it, suggest cost optimizations, and quote within 24 hours.

Which shipping terms do you support?+

We export to 50+ countries with FOB, CIF, and DDP terms, handling export documentation, packing, and logistics so your parts arrive ready to use.
